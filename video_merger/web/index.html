File type
WMV parts (joined with AsfBin) always give a .wmv file: they are never re-encoded.
The parts are deleted for good (not to the Recycle Bin), right after their merged file passed the checks: length, size and every sound and subtitle track.
In Explorer: Shift + right-click a folder, “Copy as path”, then paste it here. You can also drag folders onto VideoMerger.exe: the page opens with them added.

Programs

Not included with Video Merger. Download FFmpeg yourself (Windows builds · all systems) and put ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe next to this program or on PATH.
AsfBin, for WMV parts (radioactivepages.com): Windows only, free for non-commercial use. Put asfbin.exe next to this program or on PATH; without it, .wmv files are left alone.

What gets merged

Only WEB-DL parts (H.264 or HEVC video with AAC sound, as DMM delivers them), joined without re-encoding: same quality, about as fast as copying the files. Each join gets a chapter (“Part 1”, “Part 2”, …), and every sound and subtitle track is kept.
WMV parts (WMV or VC-1 video with WMA sound) are joined with AsfBin into one .wmv, also without re-encoding, with a marker at each join. Without AsfBin, .wmv files are left alone.
Left as they are: VP9 videos (DMM's 4K parts), Blu-ray/DVD Remuxes and rips, parts in different formats, and parts that differ in picture size, frame rate, codec or sound. A video is merged only when every part is there and the result has the right length.
DMM files are matched by the content id and part number DMM writes into each file, whatever they are called. Other files by name, within one folder: name-pt1, name part 2, name_3, ABC-123-A; and name-1, name 2, Name (iii) more when part 1 and another part are both there.
Runs on your computer only; closing the window stops Video Merger (a merge in progress is removed, the parts are never changed). Log:
